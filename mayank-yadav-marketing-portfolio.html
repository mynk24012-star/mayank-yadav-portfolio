I'm Mayank Yadav, a growth marketer, content strategist, and content writer based in India. I hold an MBA in Marketing and Finance, which means I approach campaigns the same way a good finance mind approaches a balance sheet — every rupee spent needs a clear return story.

I understand that growth isn't just traffic and impressions. It's the content that earns trust, the ad that converts at 3 AM, the retention flow that brings someone back. I focus on all three — and on connecting them so they actually compound.

I work best with founders, early-stage startups, finance brands, coaches, and personal brand builders who want sharp, honest marketing — not inflated agency language or vanity metrics.

Case 01

Finance Content Growth Campaign

Content Strategy · Finance

Problem

A finance brand had technically accurate content that wasn't gaining traction. Low engagement, low trust signals, and no clear narrative thread connecting their content to their core offer.

Approach

Rebuilt the content framework around audience pain points rather than product features. Simplified financial concepts for a non-expert audience, introduced a consistent brand voice, and aligned content topics to search and purchase intent.

Outcome

Improved content engagement and organic reach. Content began converting readers into enquiries — not just informing them. Trust signals strengthened across the brand touchpoints.

Case 02

Paid Ads & Conversion Improvement

Google Ads · Meta Ads

Problem

A brand was running paid campaigns with decent traffic but poor conversion rates. Ad spend was generating clicks that didn't result in meaningful business outcomes — a classic top-of-funnel trap.

Approach

Audited the full paid-to-landing-page journey. Tightened ad copy and audience targeting on Meta, restructured Google Search campaigns around purchase-intent keywords, and aligned landing page messaging with ad promises to reduce friction.

Outcome

Improved conversion rate from paid traffic. More budget was redirected toward performing segments, improving overall ROAS without increasing total spend.

Skills Used

Google Ads Meta Ads Conversion Optimisation Ad Copy

Case 03

Creator-Led Content Growth Sprint

Content Growth · Distribution

Problem

A content channel needed rapid, sustainable growth without relying on paid amplification. Organic reach was flat, content wasn't being discovered, and there was no playbook for scaling views.

Approach

Developed a creator-led growth strategy — selecting high-potential content formats, building a consistent publishing cadence, and optimising each piece for platform-specific discovery. Distribution was treated as a discipline, not an afterthought.

Outcome

1 million+ views achieved within 15 days. Growth was paired with conversion-focused content that turned views into tangible business outcomes — not just vanity numbers.
